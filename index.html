48-HOUR GAME JAM SPACE EXPLORATION SURVIVAL EXTRACTION 2.5D / 3D 2026

OXIE DEATH (OXIERUN)

Inspect Game Jam Key Art, Oxygen Survival Loop & Mechanics
ROLE: 2D / 3D GAME ARTIST • 48-HOUR GAME JAM (TEAM "DEADLINE IS CALLING")

OXIE DEATH (OxieRun) is a high-stakes Sci-Fi Survival & Planetary Extraction Game created in a 48-Hour Game Jam under the theme Space Exploration. Players step into the boots of an astronaut stationed on a hostile alien world where Oxygen is your most precious currency—every breath counts, and one hit from an alien monstrosity means instant death.

  • Core Extraction Gameplay Loop: Accept planetary extraction contracts at the Base Station's Quest Table, cycle through the Airlock Door, pulse the Resource Scanner (`[F]`) to ping rare minerals (Diamond, Topaz, Amethyst) and alien bio-samples on the mini-map, and return to the Drop-to-Sell Quest Table (`[Q]`) to earn Credits for Shop Terminal upgrades (movement speed, O2 tank capacity, and radar range).
  • Oxygen & Hypoxia Life-Support System: Continuous oxygen depletion outside the habitat (accelerated while sprinting), featuring visor frost and dizzying tunnel-vision warnings at critically low O2, plus portable O2 Canisters (`[E]`) for emergency field refills.
  • Dynamic 1-Hand & 2-Hand Item Visualization: Small ores, gems, and specimens are carried single-handedly in the right hand, while heavy cargo crates and Gold Chests require a realistic two-handed carrying posture.
  • Adaptive Alien Monster AI: Guarded artifacts attract lethal monsters featuring Wander patrol states, Sound & Vision range detection, 1.25x chase speed, and a 1.5x enraged speed buff when players steal alien eggs.
Tools Used: Unity Engine Blender Autodesk Maya Adobe Photoshop Procreate GitHub
