3D CHARACTER, MONSTER & PROP MODELER • UNITY

WE ARE CHOURI

INSPECT 3D ASSETS & CUTSCENE →

WE ARE CHOURI is a stylized 3D fantasy adventure built in Unity. Serving as Position Modeler, I designed and modeled the dual playable protagonists Guidi & Zeppe from 2D color concept explorations through clean 3D character topology and hand-crafted textures.

Beyond the main cast, I modeled enemy monsters, horned shrine guardians, UV-unwrapped glowing tiki lanterns, and a collection of 15 stylized dessert and fantasy props used to dress the game's surreal caverns and bridges.

I also directed and animated the in-engine Unity opening cutscene (Chouri Wake Up in Forest), bringing the characters and world lighting together in motion.

NO MORE RAT! : DOWN THE DRAIN (by MidnightParadise) is a hack-and-slash action game on Roblox where players step into the role of an exterminator hamster battling waves of mutant rats across household and underground stages.

As 3D Boss & Enemy Modeler, I created the stylized trenchcoat hamster character alongside a roster of six distinct boss and creature models—ranging from a four-armed mutant rat brute and venomous beast to a spear-wielding mounted rider and dark rat demon.

48-HOUR GAME JAM 2026 • 2.5D / 2D SURVIVAL EXPLORATION • UNITY • AWS ROADMAP

OXIE DEATH

INSPECT GAME JAM DESIGN & ART →

OXIE DEATH (OxieRun) is an adrenaline-fueled survival exploration game created under the 48-Hour Game Jam theme “Space Exploration” by team Deadline is calling (Methichai Prueksaking, Art & Design).

Players step into the boots of an astronaut contractor on a hostile alien world where Oxygen is your most precious currency. Venture beyond the research base station, scan for rare mineral artifacts, evade lethal monstrosities where one strike means instant death, and extract before your air tank runs dry.

Features dynamic one-handed vs two-handed item holding physics, low-O2 frost and tunnel vision effects, a drop-to-sell Quest Table bounty system, and an enterprise AWS Cloud multiplayer roadmap (Amazon GameLift, Cognito, AppSync WebSockets, DynamoDB).

Hi, I’m Methichai Prueksaking (AI Methichai)!

I am a 3D Artist and Game Development student at King Mongkut’s University of Technology Thonburi (KMUTT), School of Architecture and Design. Guided by the motto “Driven by imagination, shaped by art,” I specialize in turning concepts into expressive 3D characters, boss creatures, stylized props, and atmospheric game environments.

Across team and solo game productions in Unity and Roblox Studio, my workflow covers high-to-low poly modeling, ZBrush sculpting, clean UV unwrapping, hand-painted & PBR texturing in Substance 3D Painter, skeletal rigging, and shader look-development for game pipelines.
